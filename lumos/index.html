MATERIAL INTEGRITY

Built for Generations, Not Seasons

We reject ephemeral plastic manufacturing. Every LUMOS luminaire is constructed entirely from raw earth minerals—solid non-ferrous metals, quarried stone, and annealed glass—designed to be completely disassembled, serviced, and cherished indefinitely.

01

Unlacquered Raw Brass

Forged without chemical clear-coats. The metal naturally oxidizes over time, reacting to atmospheric humidity and human touch to develop an organic, irreplaceable heritage patina.

Origin: Tuscany, Italy
02

Mouth-Blown Opaline Glass

Each globe is shaped by artisanal glassblowers without industrial molds. A dual chemical acid bath gives the glass an ethereal matte texture that diffuses lumen glare into soft ambient warmth.

Origin: Bohemia, Czech Republic
03

Monolithic Italian Travertine

Extracted from historic Roman quarries. Cut from solid stone blocks and honed by hand, each base displays singular porous veining and cavernous voids that ensure no two pieces are identical.

Origin: Tivoli, Italy
