02 / Methodology

Technical Philosophy

How I approach building software for the modern web: focused on intentional restraint, sub-second execution, and resilience.

01

Precision Typography & Fluid Layouts

Interfaces should feel calm and effortless. I leverage fluid clamp scaling, proportional scale modularity, and tight typographic hierarchies so content feels balanced on an iPhone or a 32-inch studio display.

02

Zero-Dependency Vanilla Architecture

Avoiding bloated runtime layers whenever possible. Clean HTML5 semantics, modern CSS Grid/Flexbox, and resilient vanilla JavaScript ensure instant initial paint times, zero supply-chain risk, and 100/100 Lighthouse performance.

03

Applied AI & LLM Systems

As an AI Engineering Student, I actively research how foundation models integrate into user-facing software. Designing intelligent pipelines, stateful deterministic agent interactions, and context-aware interfaces.
