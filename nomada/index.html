THE ETHOS

Primitive technique, uncompromising precision.

“At Nómada, we surrendered electric induction stoves and industrial water baths in favor of the raw, unpredictable intensity of glowing native coals. Fire is not merely our heat source; it is our most expressive ingredient.”

We partner exclusively with regenerative farmers whose soil microbiology we inspect ourselves. Every vegetable is harvested within 40 kilometers, preserving the volatile aromatic oils that evaporate within hours of picking.

Matías Albarracín Chef & Co-Founder
